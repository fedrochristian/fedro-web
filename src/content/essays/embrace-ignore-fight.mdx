There are three honest postures toward the curve. They are not strategies. They
are postures — the place you stand from before you act. Pick wrong and your
strategy is incoherent. Pick right and your strategy can be argued with by
people who pick the other two.

## Embrace

Embrace says: *the curve is happening, I am going to stand near the wheel.*
It is the posture of the builder, the founder, the policy hand who joins
rather than petitions. It risks complicity with bad outcomes for the chance
to shape good ones. The embrace position is not "techno-optimism." Optimism is
a forecast. Embrace is a job.

## Ignore

Ignore says: *the curve is happening, and the curve is not the most important
thing.* It is the posture of the parent, the priest, the woodworker, the person
who refuses to be governed by the discourse. It risks being unprepared. But
ignore is not denial — it is **a refusal to be conscripted into other people's
panic.** The graph does not need your attention to advance. Tend your garden,
say the things you mean, raise the children with care.

The thing ignore must not do is curdle into incuriosity. It must keep the
quiet eye open.

## Fight

Fight says: *the curve is happening, and I do not consent to all of it.* It is
the posture of the regulator, the labor organizer, the philosopher, the
parent at the school board, the engineer who quits and tells you why. Fight is
not the same as Luddism, though it is sometimes accused of it. Fight asks:
**slow down, on whose timeline, for whose benefit, with what off-switch?**

<Callout title="The honest fight">
  An honest fight does not pretend to want the world before. It wants a world
  with friction in the right places, an actually-existing brake, and the
  political will to use it. That is rarer than it sounds.
</Callout>

## Why "even nothing is an answer"

This is the part most readers want to skip. There is a fourth position called
*default*. It is the position of the person who insists they have not chosen
yet, while their week continues to embed them in one of the three postures
above. **Default is a vote cast by inaction.** It usually votes embrace.

The publication's argument is not that you must pick one. It is that you
should know which one you are standing in, and live like it on purpose.
